OUR PROCESS

How It Works

Getting solar is simple. We make the process easy, transparent and stress-free from start to finish.

  1. We look at your power bills, your roof and how you use energy, then recommend the right size for you.

    • Free, no-obligation site assessment
    • Roof, shade and switchboard check
    • Usage review from your power bill

  2. You get a custom design and a clear, fixed-price quote with your expected savings and payback.

    • Panel layout and equipment choices
    • Savings and payback estimate
    • We handle your lines company application

  3. Our certified installers fit the system, connect it and test everything before they hand it over.

    • Certified installers and a tidy site
    • Full safety and performance testing
    • A walkthrough of how it all works

  4. Track your system in the app. We watch its performance and we are one call away if you need us.

    • Live monitoring in the app
    • Yearly performance check
    • Workmanship and product warranties

Most home systems cost roughly NZ$8,000 to NZ$20,000 installed, depending on the size, your roof type and whether you add a battery. Commercial systems are quoted project by project. The savings calculator above gives you a quick estimate, and your fixed-price quote after a free site assessment gives the exact figure.

Most homes reach payback in about 6 to 9 years. It depends on how much power you use during the day, your region and your electricity price. Businesses that use most of their power in daytime hours often pay back faster.

There is currently no national solar rebate. Your savings come from the power you generate and use yourself, plus whatever your retailer pays for the power you export. Offers can change, so we will tell you about any that apply when we prepare your quote.

Most retailers offer a buy-back rate for exported power. Rates vary between retailers and are usually lower than the price you pay to buy electricity, so it pays to use as much of your solar power at home as you can. We can compare options for you.

Most home systems are installed in one to two days. The whole journey, from your site assessment to switching on, usually takes a few weeks. The exact time depends on your design, equipment availability and approval from your lines company.

Your local lines company must approve any system that connects to the grid, and we handle that application for you. Many roof-mounted systems do not need a building consent, but it depends on your roof and your council, so we check this during the design stage.

Yes. Panels still generate on overcast days, just at a lower output, and New Zealand gets plenty of sun through most of the year. We design your system for your region and roof, so your savings estimate already allows for the seasonal ups and downs.

No. A battery stores extra solar power for the evening and can keep key circuits running in an outage if your system supports it. Many homes start with panels only. We can design your system to be battery-ready so you can add one later.

Yes. A smart EV charger can use your solar power while the sun is out, so you drive on energy you made yourself. We can install the charger with your system or add it later.

Quality panels usually come with a 25-year performance warranty, and inverters often have 10 years of cover or more. You also get a workmanship warranty from us. The exact terms are set out in your quote.
